# Q2.11: predictions written before training models B, C and D

Model B (mirror and rotation augmentation) will lower C_M and C_R and bring E_M and E_R close to E(5), because those failures come from the digits' appearance, but it will not help at 2x or 3x. Model C (speed augmentation) will fix much of the 2x failure, because 2x motion (about 6.6 pixels per frame) is inside model A's 8-pixel reach and was only unfamiliar, but it will still do badly at 3x (about 10 pixels per frame). Only model D, with a 12-pixel reach and speed augmentation, will also fix 3x, while neither C nor D will change C_M or C_R much.
