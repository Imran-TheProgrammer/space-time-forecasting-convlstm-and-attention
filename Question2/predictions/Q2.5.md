# Q2.5: predictions written before running any experiment in "Testing the Baseline"

Inputs to these predictions: model A's reach estimate (Q2.3: 8 pixels per time step) and Figure 1
(digits move in all directions; median speed 3.32 pixels per frame).
No mirror, rotation or speed-up test had been run when this was written.

**Mirror test.** Figure 1(a) shows digits moving in every direction, so mirrored motion is
motion model A has already seen. Only the look of the digits is new (a mirrored 2 is not a 2).
I expect model A to mostly pass: the digits should still move the right way, but their shapes may
blur or drift back towards normal-looking digits. So E_M should be a little higher than E(5), and
C_M small but not zero.

**Rotation test.** The motion is again familiar, but rotated digits look even less like the
training digits than mirrored ones. I expect model A to do worse than on the mirror test
(C_R > C_M and E_R > E_M), while the digits still move in roughly the right direction.

**Speed.** The median speed is about 3.3 pixels per frame, so 2x is about 6.6 and 3x about
10 pixels per frame. Model A can move information at most 8 pixels per step. At 2x the motion is
still inside this reach, but the model never saw such fast motion in training, so I expect some
extra error but no breakdown. At 3x most motion is beyond the reach, so I expect model A to break
down there: blurred digits that stay near their old position or fade away.
